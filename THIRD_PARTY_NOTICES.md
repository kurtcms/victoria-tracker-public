## WHO Child Growth Standards

The growth calculations use the World Health Organization’s Child Growth Standards, expanded weight-for-age tables, female and male, 2006; sources verified November 14, 2026. The source URLs and expected file hashes are recorded in `ios/VictoriaTracker/Resources/Growth/SOURCE.json`.

The numerical reference CSVs are not distributed in this source repository. Run `python3 scripts/import_who_reference.py` locally only after reviewing the applicable WHO terms. The script downloads official workbooks and extracts the age/L/M/S reference parameters.
